Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026  ·  Version 2.1

1. Overview

Telco X is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use our Service Availability portal. By using this service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

When you search for an address or check service availability, we may collect:

  • Address search queries you enter into the search field
  • Location identifiers used to retrieve service data
  • Browser type, operating system, and general device information
  • Anonymised usage analytics (pages visited, session duration)

We do not collect account credentials, payment details, or personally identifiable information through this portal.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Return accurate service availability results for the address you search
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the portal
  • Generate anonymised aggregate statistics about service coverage

4. Data Storage and Retention

Search queries are processed in memory and are not stored beyond your session. Anonymised analytics data is retained for up to 12 months before automatic deletion. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies

This portal uses session cookies strictly necessary for functionality. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some features may not work as expected.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Australian privacy law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold. To exercise these rights, please use the Contact us page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, contact our Privacy Officer at the contact page or write to Telco X, Privacy Officer, GPO Box 1234, Melbourne VIC 3000.
